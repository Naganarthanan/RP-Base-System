What the baseline hashes

Hashed on-chain: certificate ID, name, programme, grade, issue date. Not hashed: logo, seal, signature, QR code, file metadata. This is the deliberate gap the manipulation attack (C1) measures and the defense (C2) closes. Per-field hashes are unsalted, which is the surface for the inference attack (C3) and its defense (C4).
